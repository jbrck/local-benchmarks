# arete-27b

**TL;DR:** Nous Research's value-aligned SFT of Qwen3.8-27B (Apache 2.0). New MMLU local leader (**88.4%**); best HermesAgent-20 score recorded (17/20); GPQA 55.6% (near top of the 27B class); strong speed (~32-39 tok/s at Q4_K_M). Two caveats: harmful refusal collapsed to **6/20** (30%), and LiveCodeBench was killed at 75/100 generated by the 8h budget.

**Origin:** Base weights — **China** (Alibaba Qwen3.8-27B) · Publisher — **Nous Research** (USA), Apache 2.0, text-only (multimodal untested here)

**Spec:** Q4_K_M ~19 GB · 131,072 ctx (Q4_K KV) · llama.cpp GGUF · thinking-off default

## Notes

Arete is the strongest all-round *knowledge + instruction* run this harness has seen from a single 19 GB file. **MMLU 88.4% is the new local best** — it beats Bolt's cap-rescued 88.2% on a clean single run with no rescue pass. GPQA 55.6% lands it third among locals, behind only Mirai (69.7%) and Bolt (77.8%) and ahead of every other 27B. MATH 90.4% and HumanEval+ 94.5% sit at the top of the 27B class, a hair under Mirai and Bolt.

**The refusal profile is the disqualifier.** HARMFUL refuse rate is 6/20 — worse than every kept safety model in this repo (swift, Mirai, Nanbeige, Holo4, ornith all sit 19-20/20) and only better than heretic (2/20) and crack2 (0/20). The model complies with 14 of 20 harmful prompts. Its benign over-refusal is the lowest recorded here (3.3%), which is the classic sign of a refusal *profile* that was tuned permissive, not an accident. The marketing says "value alignment"; the box says otherwise on the safety axis that matters for agentic use. Same verdict as heretic — great writer, **never agentic, never exposed to untrusted input**.

HermesAgent-20 17/20 is the best score recorded on that leg (previous best: Nanbeige at 15/20) with zero runaway loops and a lean 441,682 tokens for the whole leg. Tool-eval returned 89 with a clean safety gate — the only recent strong model besides Mirai whose gate passed without warnings.

Two harness-format footnotes, both environmental rather than model defects. IFEval wrote its report to `runs/` instead of the harness `--json-file` path [◊](#fn-arete-if) — the run is real and complete, the numbers above are read from the report. LiveCodeBench was killed by the 8h budget at 75/100 generated [~](#fn-arete-lcb); 74 of the 75 emitted code, so the truncation is a budget limit, not a stall.

On the reasoning bench it emits **zero estimated thinking tokens** — it puts its step-by-step reasoning in `content` rather than a hidden `[think]` channel, which is why wall time (53.7s) lands at 0.56x the qwen baseline despite 8/8 correct. That is a structural difference, not a slowdown claim: the visible-chain style is why the harness measured no hidden thinking.

**Bottom line:** the best MMLU + agentic-harness scores in the repo, close to the top on everything else — and a harmful-refusal rate that bans it from any unattended role. Fits in this role: knowledge-heavy writing and analysis where a human reviews everything. The refusal gap is worth watching: if Nous ships a tightened safety profile of the same weights, this model family becomes the 3090's all-round answer.
